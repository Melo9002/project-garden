# Current design

## Implemented
One garden, four independently wandering geometric inhabitants named Earth, Fire, Wind and Water. Pause and three fixed camera positions. Movement has no care consequences yet. All creatures hover above the scenery; the pond is decorative and does not block movement.

## Planned experiments
Environmental change should produce readable behavior and reaction faces. Start with four reactions: happy, unamused, curious, distressed. Test one friendly social encounter. Cooldowns prevent reaction spam; presentation consumes semantic reaction events rather than deciding simulation rules.

Shared pupal form develops toward four elemental forms. Exact evolution thresholds remain undecided. Temperature, water and light are provisional controls, not verified PS1 mechanics. No mortality or food rules are assumed.

## Presentation
Simple 3D diorama with 2D pixie sprites in a later batch. No elaborate scenery required. Replace geometric views without changing individual state. Frame artwork at actual gameplay scale before refining it.
