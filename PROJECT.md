# Vision

An original virtual-pet garden inspired by the PS1 Pixygarden. Tend mysterious creatures, observe their responses, discover development, and watch them interact. Quiet miniature environments with a few fixed viewpoints. Begin small; eventually support multiple locations and planets.

Pedro has programming experience and an Information Systems degree. Documentation should emphasize Godot specifics, ownership, contracts and tradeoffs rather than programming basics.

Pedro draws final 2D artwork. Codex supplies temporary art references and simple 3D scenery. Approved direction: angular anime shapes, expressive faces, restrained cel shading and simple silhouettes. Earth and Fire are feminine tomboys; Wind has paired feathered ears; Water has fins. All share a pale clothed pupal form in a blue orb. The ship assistant is a possible v0.2 feature.
